# OneWorld

## Description

OneWorld is a solidarity-based web platform designed to facilitate the social and professional integration of refugees and displaced people.  
The platform aims to connect refugees with volunteers, organizations, and available opportunities such as job offers, training programs, events, and social support.

Through a simple, inclusive, and user-friendly interface, OneWorld promotes mutual aid, access to essential resources, and community engagement to support successful integration.

## 1 Project Objectives

- Facilitate the social and professional integration of refugees  
- Provide a safe and accessible digital space for support and information  
- Centralize offers, trainings, and events in one platform  
- Encourage solidarity and community participation  
- Apply web development concepts learned during academic coursework  

---
## 2 Project Modules

* **Users**: registration, authentication, and profile management, with social login via Google or Facebook  
* **Offers**: publishing job offers, training opportunities, housing assistance, financial aid, health and social services, legal assistance, and volunteering opportunities. Email notifications confirm applications  
* **Publications**: sharing posts, personal experiences, and useful information to maintain a safe and respectful community  
* **Complaints**: submitting complaints or feedback regarding platform usage or integration issues. Administrators are notified via email  
* **Trainings & Learning**: accessing educational content and professional development programs. Certificates are sent via email upon completion  
* **Events**: creating and managing community events, workshops, cultural activities, and awareness sessions

## 5 Équipe
**Zayati Eya** - Module User: The Users module manages user registration, account creation, authentication, account management, and moderation (deletion and banning). Users can register and sign in using **Google or Facebook**, providing a fast, secure, and user-friendly authentication process.

**Smaoui Dhafer** - Module offers: 
 The Offers module allows organizations, partners, and administrators to publish various types of offers intended to support refugees in their integration process. Users can browse, search, and filter offers based on their needs and interests. **Artificial intelligence** is used to automatically analyze user profiles and suggest relevant offers. When a user submits an application form for an offer, an automatic email notification is sent to confirm the submission and ensure effective communication.
